Can AI write a choose your own adventure story?

Yes, AI can write a choose your own adventure story, and it is genuinely useful for drafting branches, but it needs a human editor. Tools like ChatGPT and Claude can generate scenes, invent choices, and even output a structured branching file if you prompt them carefully. What they struggle with is consistency across many branches: they lose track of which path the player took, forget earlier choices, and sometimes create dead ends or contradictions. So AI is best treated as a fast first-draft collaborator, not a hands-off author. You still map the structure, check that every branch connects, and rewrite for voice.

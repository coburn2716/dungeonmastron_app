Dungeon Mastron

Design worlds. Shape stories. Play the outcome.

Dungeon Mastron is the complete storytelling experience, bringing together writing, visual world-building, and play into one cohesive flow.

With the help of the AI Companion, you can draft stories, explore visual styles, or hand-craft every detail yourself. Build your game in the editor,
then play it instantly in the web player — or load it onto the physical console and experience it the old-fashioned way.

Console & Web-Player

One Platform. Two Ways to Play.

The web is the invitation. The physical console is the aspiration. Same format—two experiences.

Web Player

Play in seconds, no installs, no setup. Download a game file and open it in the Web Player to explore new games, test your own creations, and share adventures with a single link.

Creation & Evolution

From a blank page, you write the story and shape the world.

Define its choices, give it a name, put your label on the cartridge, and make it your own.

Dungeon Mastron is built as an ongoing platform, shaped by the stories people create and the needs that emerge from them. New tools, systems, and ways to play are added over time, guided by the community and the kinds of adventures being made.
